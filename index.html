ABOUT ME

Researcher. Educator. Learner.

I am a PhD researcher in Computer Science with research interests in Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Medical Imaging, and Generative AI.

My current research focuses on AI-driven medical image reconstruction, particularly the development of physics-guided diffusion models for accelerated MRI reconstruction. My work explores how generative deep learning can be combined with MRI acquisition physics to improve reconstruction quality, robustness, and efficiency.

Alongside research, I have experience in teaching Computer Science and mentoring students through programming, data structures, algorithms, and machine learning projects.

I am particularly interested in developing trustworthy and practically useful AI systems while making complex technical concepts accessible through effective teaching and hands-on learning.

CURRENT RESEARCH

Physics-guided Diffusion Models for Accelerated MRI Reconstruction

My current research investigates diffusion-based generative models for reconstructing high-quality MRI images from undersampled k-space measurements. The work combines data-driven learning with MRI acquisition physics to improve reconstruction fidelity, robustness and computational efficiency.
